How to play

Klondike
  • Build the four foundations at the top from ace to king, one suit each.
  • In the seven columns, put cards in descending order with colours alternating, a red 6 on a black 7. You can move a run of cards together.
  • Only a king, or a run starting with one, can go in an empty column.
  • Tap the deck to turn cards over. When it runs out, tap the empty space to turn the pile back over. There's no limit on passes.
FreeCell
  • Build the four foundations on the right from ace to king, one suit each.
  • In the eight columns, put cards in descending order with colours alternating. Any card can go in an empty column.
  • Each of the four free cells on the left holds one card while you work.
  • A run moves together if there's room to move it a card at a time: one card for each empty free cell, plus one, doubled for each empty column.
Spider
  • Build down in the ten columns, a 6 on any 7. Only a run of one suit moves together.
  • A run from king to ace in one suit clears away by itself. Clear all eight to win.
  • Tap the deck to deal a row, a card onto every column. Every column needs at least one card first.
  • Any card or run of one suit can go in an empty column.

In every game, tap a card to send it to the best place, or drag it where you want it. On a keyboard: Space uses the deck, Z undoes, H gives a hint and N starts a new game.

No ads, no account. Your game and your record stay on this device, and once it has loaded it works with no signal. Add it to your home screen to keep it like an app.
